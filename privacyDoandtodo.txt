# Privacy Policy for Do & ToDo

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

We built the **Do & ToDo** mobile application as an ad-supported app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

## 1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
**Do & ToDo** does not require an account, login, or cloud registration. All tasks, completion states, custom text size preferences, and motion settings you create are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's local storage (Room Database and DataStore). We do not transmit, view, or store your personal to-do lists on any external server.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
To keep **Do & ToDo** free to use, the app displays banner advertisements provided by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC).

While we do not collect personal data directly, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information to serve and measure ads, including:
* **Device Identifiers:** Such as your Android Advertising ID (AAID).
* **Approximate Location:** Inferred from your IP address for regional ad relevance and compliance.
* **App Interactions & Diagnostics:** Ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, and performance metrics.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:
* [Google Privacy & Terms – How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Your Choices and Opt-Out Controls
* **Reset or Opt Out of Personalized Ads:** You can reset your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads**).
* **Delete Local App Data:** You can permanently erase all locally stored tasks and preferences at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

## 4. Children's Privacy
**Do & ToDo** is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
